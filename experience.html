Performance and writing

Before housing advocacy and alongside it, she has worked as a singer, actress and playwright. In 2008 she won a BRIO Award — Bronx Recognizes Its Own — for vocal performance, and the following June headlined the free concert marking the Bronx Council on the Arts’ twentieth anniversary of recognising independent artists.

That work is not separate from the advocacy. The historical theater teaching, the dramaturgy, the Motown thesis and the #HerHousing podcast are all the same instinct: that the way a story gets told determines who is believed.
