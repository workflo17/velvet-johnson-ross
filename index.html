What she works on

4 areas

Voucher access and value

Expanding who qualifies for rental assistance and raising benefit levels so a voucher can actually reach a market rent — the gap that leaves people holding a voucher and still living in shelter.

HAVP · CityFHEPS · Section 8 / HCV · Emergency Housing Vouchers

Source-of-income discrimination

Landlord and broker refusal of vouchers — illegal in New York City and routine anyway. Her work here is about evidence: giving voucher holders a paper trail that makes refusal costly.

Landlord acceptance · Broker conduct · Enforcement

Eviction prevention and recovery

Both sides of the eviction line — keeping households in place, and bridging the period after a family has already been displaced, when almost no program is designed for them.

Illegal eviction · Habitability · Post-eviction support

Housing, race and gender

The compounding barriers — an eviction record, a voucher, damaged credit — that lock women of color out of the same neighborhoods their families were redlined out of a generation ago.

Redlining legacy · Eviction records · Credit screening
