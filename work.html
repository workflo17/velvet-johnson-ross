2023 · Oct 6 City Hall

Quoted by the City on ending credit checks for voucher holders

City of New York · HPD — billed as Founder, The Eviction Fund of New York City

New York City eliminated credit-check requirements for voucher holders entering city-subsidized affordable housing, opening access for roughly 4,000 families a year. The City’s announcement carried her statement: This opens up a whole new possibility for a better tomorrow for unhoused individuals and families who have been locked out of housing for so long based on their credit.

She added that by removing credit as a determining factor for housing eligibility, society can address the systemic barriers that have perpetuated homelessness.

Source City of New York

Gothamist — “Nearly 8K NYC households could lose rent aid as federal program runs out of money”

2021 · Feb 9 Testimony

Testimony for the Housing Access Voucher Program

New York State Executive Budget Hearing on Social Services · Senate Finance and Assembly Ways & Means Committees

Testifying as a leader at Neighbors Together, she made the case for passing and fully funding HAVP — a statewide rental assistance program paying fair market rent — and put a number on the first year.

A3701 / S2804 — HAVP A2617 / S4050 — COVID-19 Housing Relief $500M year one $2.25B total ask

She also backed the Invest in Our New York revenue platform as the way to pay for it, and framed the stakes around the 92,000-plus people then living in New York shelters.

Outcome

The Housing Access Voucher Program launched statewide on March 1, 2026 — New York’s first statewide rental assistance program — with an initial $50 million allocation. Advocates continue to press for $250 million annually.
